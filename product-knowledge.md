## About

Kulu builds agentic infrastructure for post-sales teams.

Customer onboarding shapes activation, adoption, retention, expansion, and trust. High-quality onboarding is still limited by calendar capacity, headcount, and manual processes.

Principles:

- Every customer deserves a guided start.
- AI should help work get done.
- Humans should focus where they matter most.
- Trust has to be built in.

Founders: exited founders, AI PhDs, and operators from fast-growing European technology companies.

What it offers customer teams: guide more accounts through onboarding with consistency, context, and control.

## Shared failure path

Each use-case page shows the same drop-off:

1. Signup
2. Setup friction
3. Unanswered questions
4. Account goes quiet

The stated causes are that setup takes longer than expected, evaluation questions slow momentum, and intent is hard to read from product activity alone.

## Admin onboarding

https://heykulu.ai/use-cases/admin-onboarding

Help every admin get their workspace ready for launch. Kulu guides admins through setup, configuration, integrations, permissions, and team rollout so the account starts with the right foundation.

Problem: admins turn a new product into something their team can use. Incomplete configuration, missed integrations, or unclear permissions slow rollout before users start.

What Kulu does: walks admins through required setup steps, answers configuration questions, and captures blockers so the account is ready to roll out.

Flow:

1. Admin starts setup. Account created.
2. Kulu setup session. Live guided walkthrough.
3. Configuration completed. Workspace ready.
4. Blockers captured. Flagged for follow-up.
5. Team launch. Rollout begins.

Outcome: launch every account with setup finished, blockers removed, and the team prepared for a stronger first experience.

## Trial conversion

https://heykulu.ai/use-cases/trial-conversion

Help trial users reach value before the window closes. Kulu guides trial users through setup, answers evaluation questions, and captures buying signals so the team knows which accounts are ready for follow-up.

Problem: trial users often sign up with real interest and still do not reach value on their own. Setup takes effort, questions come up, and the account can go quiet before the team knows they need help.

What Kulu does: meets the user inside the trial, helps them complete setup, answers questions in the moment, and captures the context the team needs.

Flow:

1. Trial starts. Account created.
2. Kulu setup session. Live guided call.
3. Activation milestone. Key step completed.
4. Signals captured. Intent and blockers.
5. Team follow-up. Routed with context.

Outcome: more trials reach value faster, and follow-up goes to the right accounts with context.

## End-user onboarding

https://heykulu.ai/use-cases/end-user-onboarding

Help every user reach value. Raise adoption by guiding each user through a 1-on-1 call.

Problem: an invite does not mean someone knows how to use the product. New users need context, guidance, and confidence before the product becomes part of their daily workflow.

What Kulu does: helps each user understand their role, learn the relevant parts of the product, and complete the first actions that make the product useful.

Flow:

1. User accepts invite. Account created.
2. Kulu orientation session. Live guided intro.
3. Role-based guidance. Personalised to the user.
4. First workflow completed. Core action taken.
5. Adoption signal captured. Ready for the next step.

Outcome: invited users become active users, oriented, through their first workflow, with confidence from day one.

## Technical product support

https://heykulu.ai/use-cases/technical-product-support

Get users unstuck on product questions in real time. Kulu helps customers troubleshoot product issues, understand configuration, answer integration questions, and route complex cases to the team with the context they need.

Problem: product questions, integration issues, permission errors, and configuration problems stall work. Without quick, clear help, the team also lacks the context to resolve the issue.

What Kulu does: asks diagnostic questions, checks against approved product knowledge, suggests clear next steps, and escalates when a human expert is needed.

Flow:

1. Customer asks for help. Account created.
2. Kulu investigates. Root cause explored.
3. Suggested next step. Step-by-step guidance.
4. Issue classified. Severity assessed.
5. Human escalation if needed. Routed with context.

Outcome: customers get technical answers faster, and the team can step in with context when the issue needs a person.
